Diablo IV

Diablo IV is Blizzard's dark action RPG, released in June 2023. Players explore the open world of Sanctuary, a land caught in the middle of the eternal war between the High Heavens and the Burning Hells, and grow their hero through loot, skills, and dungeons.

The Burning Hells

The Burning Hells are home to the Prime Evils, Diablo, Mephisto, and Baal, and the Lesser Evils who serve beneath them. Their endless war with the angels of the High Heavens is why Sanctuary is never safe for long.
